            How it's made

            Meta tracker

            Live from the public deadlock-api.com stats service, which collects match results from Valve's servers. If it can't be reached, the page shows a saved copy from 23 Nov 2026.

            Skill tiers

            60% Valve's in-game complexity rating (1–4), 40% the deadlock-boosting difficulty guide, plus 1 point for heroes that big sites keep calling high-skill-ceiling, such as Yamato, Shiv and Ivy. Then real matches adjust it by up to 2 points: heroes whose players win far more after 50 games than in their first 10 (like Vyper and Baba) move up, heroes you're good with straight away (like Paige and Seven) move down.
