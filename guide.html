Hero guides

How to play every hero: what they do, what they're good and bad at, their abilities, and how to win your lane. Pick a hero.

Where this comes from

Game data

Role, playstyle, stats, weapon numbers and ability text come straight from the game's files. For newer heroes without an official playstyle line, it's summed up from their ability descriptions.

High-rank matches

Skill order, power spike and counter items come from Oracle-and-up games. Lane partners and matchups come from all ranks over the last two weeks.

Strengths and weaknesses

Each stat is compared with all 44 heroes. Top quarter counts as a strength, bottom quarter as a weakness.
