Hero builds

One build for every hero, put together from what Oracle-and-up players buy and win with. Rebuilt every week. Pick a hero.

How the builds are made

What good players buy

For every hero I took all item purchases from Oracle-and-up games over the last three weeks. Each phase starts from the items most of those players buy.

Nudged toward what wins

Items get extra weight when they win more than other popular items in the same tier. Items bought much later than usual lose some weight, because those mostly show up in long games that were already being won.

Order and phases

Lane is tier 1 items bought in the first 12 minutes, then tier 2 (Core), tier 3 (Mid game) and tier 4 (Late game). Inside each phase, items are in the order players usually buy them.

The game reasoning

Each item's reason comes from its real in-game stats and effect, checked against the hero's kit: which abilities scale with spirit power, whether the hero fights with gun or abilities, and whether it's lane or late game. Upgrade paths come from the game's own item data.

Skill order and matchups

The skill order is the ability high-rank players max first, with how often they win. Matchup items are the ones that win clearly more than usual for this hero when a specific hard enemy is in the game.
