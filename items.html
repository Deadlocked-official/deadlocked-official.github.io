How items are graded

Same-tier comparison

Each item's win rate is compared with the popular items of its own tier, because expensive items always look strong: the winning team can afford them.

Late buys count less

Items bought much later than usual for their tier lose half a point per minute, since those buys mostly happen in long games that were already won.

Read it with care

Defensive items like Metal Skin get bought when you're already behind, so they grade low even when they're the right call. Grades say what wins on average, not what your game needs.
